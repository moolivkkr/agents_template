## Narrowed findings

**AI-05 (PARTIAL brief not passed as a gap) — MEDIUM.** "The arbitrator isn't told" overstates it.
`debate_researcher.md:99-102` makes every brief carry a "Confidence in Evidence" section
(strong/moderate/weak counts) and `:111` tells the researcher to write "Limited data available for
this option"; `debate_arbitrator.md:54` and `:72` have the judge read the brief and note unevidenced
claims, and `:104` caps confidence at MEDIUM when the decisive claim is unverifiable. What survives:
the moderator treats a `PARTIAL` return as a finished result (`debate_moderator.md:107-109`), only a
missing file becomes a recorded gap (`:119-121`), and no rule caps confidence or writes INCOMPLETE
when one option's evidence is materially thinner — and the 8-anchors (`debate-protocol.md:243-261`)
do mechanically reward the better-documented option.

**AI-09 (concurrent subagent limit) — MEDIUM.** The limit and the "don't retry" text are confirmed
verbatim from Anthropic's docs (below), and the moderator's step 4 has no case for a spawn error (an
Agent-tool error isn't a child return). But: 20 running is allowed and the 21st fails, so five
moderators with three options each (20 slots) fit; the failure needs five moderators with four
options or six with three, dispatched in one message. The 10-per-phase cap allows that; it isn't the
common case, and the eval T-007 (3 debates × 2 options = 9 slots) never reaches it. And the failure
is visible, not silent: the moderator records a gap, the verdict becomes INCOMPLETE/LOW, and
`debate-status.py` lists it for review. Whether nested subagents count toward the session limit is
not stated on the page ([unverified]; the limit is phrased per session, so plausibly yes).

**AI-19 (no request digest in the verdict) — MEDIUM.** No hash, timestamp or mtime check exists
anywhere (grep over `debate-status.py`, the moderator, the protocol and `remember.sh`). But
`debate-status.py:119-122` already marks a verdict invalid when its option id is no longer among the
request's options, so a re-file that removes or re-ids the winning option is caught and blocks the
gate. What slips through is a re-file under the same slug with changed labels, context or an added
option while the winning id survives.
